Privacy policy

This policy covers the apps and tools published by HieuBigby that let you sign in with a Google account.

What is accessed

Only the Google account data you agree to on Google's sign-in screen, and only to provide the features you use.

Where the data goes

Data is kept on your own devices and in your own Google account. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train models. Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
